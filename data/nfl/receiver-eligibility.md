# Receiver Eligibility and Illegal Touching

## Eligible positions

For a forward pass thrown from behind the line of scrimmage, eligible offensive receivers include
the players on the two ends of the line and players positioned at least one yard behind the line.
They must wear eligible numbers or have reported an eligible status when the rules permit reporting.
All defensive players are eligible receivers.

An offensive formation must have at least seven players on the line. The interior linemen between
the two ends are not eligible receivers merely because they wear an eligible number.

## Reporting as eligible

A player wearing an ordinarily ineligible number can report to the referee as eligible for a play.
The player must then occupy an eligible position in a legal formation. Reporting changes the
player's declared status; it does not excuse an illegal alignment.

## Going out of bounds

An eligible offensive receiver who goes out of bounds before or during a pass becomes ineligible to
be the first player to touch that pass, even after reestablishing inbounds. If the receiver is the
first to touch or catch the pass after returning, illegal touching applies.

Whether the receiver went out voluntarily or was legally forced out can affect other parts of the
play, but neither circumstance automatically restores eligibility to touch the pass first.
