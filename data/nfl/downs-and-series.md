# Downs, Series, and the Line to Gain

## The purpose of a down

A down is one continuous period of action beginning with a legal snap or free kick and ending when
the ball becomes dead. The down number records how many opportunities the team in possession has
used in its current series.

## Earning a new series

The offense ordinarily receives a series of four scrimmage downs. It earns a new series when the
ball is declared dead in its possession at or beyond the line to gain. A defensive penalty can also
produce a new series by moving the ball to the line to gain or by awarding an automatic first down.

The forward-most point of the ball when it becomes dead is used when measuring whether the line to
gain was reached.

## Turnover on downs

If the offense has not reached the line to gain by the end of fourth down, the opponent receives a
new series. The next possession begins at the dead-ball spot after any accepted penalties are
enforced.

A change of possession during a play can produce a new series for the team that possesses the ball
at the end of the down. Interceptions and recovered fumbles are common examples.
