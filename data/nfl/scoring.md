# Scoring Plays

## Point values

A touchdown scores six points, a field goal scores three, and a safety scores two. After a
touchdown, a successful kick or a safety during the try scores one point, while a touchdown during
the try scores two points.

## Touchdowns and the goal line

A runner scores when possessing the ball on, above, or beyond the plane of the opponent's goal line.
The ball becomes dead as soon as the touchdown requirement is satisfied. A runner crossing the goal
line is different from a receiver completing a catch in the end zone: the receiver must first
complete the catch requirements.

## Field goals and tries

A field goal scores when a legal kick passes completely over the crossbar and between the uprights,
or above the uprights, without first touching the ground or an offensive player. A try is one
scrimmage down after a touchdown during which either team may score under the try rules, and the game
clock does not run.

## Safeties

A safety commonly occurs when a team provides the impetus that puts the ball behind its own goal
line and the ball becomes dead there in that team's possession, or when its foul is enforced from a
spot in its own end zone. Possession, impetus, the dead-ball reason, and the enforcement spot are all
needed to resolve less typical safety scenarios.
